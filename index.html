The contribution graph is extremely overrated. Yes, it looks cool, but conceptually it means nothing. It's not something you should work on intentionally, and not something to be proud of, whether you never miss a day or ship hundreds of commits a day. Commit count is a terrible metric, just like lines of code written: it says nothing about quality.

A lot of people build customizations of the GitHub contribution graph, or even whole projects around it, to flex with the numbers. I did the opposite. Forget the number of contributions. I don't care. This is my small game, Touch the Grass, where the goal is to avoid making commits.

Yeah, okay, it's really just an excuse to hide the fact that my public contribution graph shows about two commits every three months. Hehe.

A tribute to Snake. You are a snake of empty days inside a contribution graph that runs on Conway's Game of Life. Steer with ←↑↓→ / WASD or tap. Outlined cells are born next generation, dim cells die. Nothing can be born under you, so the danger is ahead: steer around the green and don't bite your tail. Eat grass to grow. Stay grey until 23:59. M toggles sound.
